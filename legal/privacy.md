# Privacy policy

Sunstone: Dusk Run ("the game") is made by Pranta Dutta. This policy says what the game collects, why, and how to have it deleted.

Last updated: 4 October 2026.

## What the game collects

**Your account.** When you first open the game it creates an anonymous account for you with Google Firebase Authentication. It has a random ID and no personal details. If you choose "Sign in with Google" (Android), "Sign in with Apple" (iPhone and iPad) or sign in with an email and password, that is linked to it so your progress can follow you to another device. With Google, or with an email and password, we keep your email address. With Apple we receive an ID and, if you choose to share it, your email address or an Apple private relay address. Passwords are held by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see or store them.

**Your runner name.** The game gives you a random name, such as "Swift Jaguar 42", which you can change. It is shown to other players on the ranks.

**Your runs.** For each run: the distance, sun-drops gathered, flares used, how long it lasted, how far into the night it went, what ended it, and when. These are used for the ranks and to keep play fair.

**Your progress.** Your save (records, glyphs, sun-drops, what you've bought with them, settings) is stored on our server so you can restore it on a new phone.

**How the game is played.** Small events such as "a run started", "a glyph was claimed" or "a purchase was made", with the time. They help us improve the game. They don't include your name or email.

**Purchases.** If you buy something, Google Play or Apple's App Store handles the payment. We receive the product and the store's proof of purchase (an order number and purchase token from Google, or a signed transaction from Apple), which we check so the item is granted once. We never see your payment details.

**Ads.** The game shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and device information to show ads and measure them. Where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent before any ad is shown, and you can change your choice later under Settings, Ad privacy. On iPhone and iPad, the advertising ID is only used if you allow tracking when iOS asks. See Google's policy at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

The game does not collect your contacts, location, photos or files.

## How it's used and shared

We use this information to run the game: to sign you in, keep your progress, show the ranks, grant purchases and improve the game. We don't sell it. It is shared only with the services that make the game work: Google Firebase (sign-in), Apple (Sign in with Apple), Google Play and the App Store (purchases), Google AdMob (ads) and the server that hosts the game's data.

## Keeping and deleting it

Your data is kept while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the game: Settings, Account, Delete account. That removes your runs, ranks, save, events and purchase records from our server and deletes your sign-in account. Purchases already made stay recorded by Google Play or the App Store.

You can also ask for deletion by email (below). We will do it within 30 days.

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear here and in the game, with a new date.
